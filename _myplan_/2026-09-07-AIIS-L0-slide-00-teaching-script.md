# Slide 00 — Welcome to AIIS
## AI and Information Security
### 人工智慧與資訊安全

## Purpose

Slide 00 is the entrance to the entire AIIS course, not just the opening of one lesson. It establishes course identity, the persistent semester story, and the student mindset before Slide 01 begins the conceptual teaching.

Do not start with definitions of AI, cybersecurity, ML, tools, or historical revolutions. Slide 00 should answer only:

1. What course are we entering?
2. What kind of journey will we take?
3. What is the central relationship between AI capability and security discipline?

## Core Course Statement

> **AI is the capability. Information Security is the discipline.**

Chinese teaching interpretation:

> AI 讓系統能做更多事情；資訊安全讓我們知道這些能力應該如何被理解、控制、驗證與保護。

## Spokesperson / 煥哥 Role

Role: **HOST / GUIDE**.

Suggested pose:
- welcoming open-hand gesture;
- stands beside the AIIS title and semester journey;
- confident, friendly, professional;
- not holding hacking tools or code yet;
- gaze guides students into the course roadmap.

Narrative role:

```text
L0: HOST / GUIDE
L1: BUILDER
L2: PROJECT LEAD
```

Slide 00 should visually establish that the same spokesperson will accompany the student through the semester.

## Teacher Opening Script

Suggested opening:

> 歡迎來到 AIIS——Artificial Intelligence and Information Security，人工智慧與資訊安全。
>
> 這門課不是先上半學期 AI、再上半學期資安，也不是把很多熱門工具放在一起介紹。
>
> 我們會一直圍繞同一個問題：當 AI 讓軟體、資料與系統可以做越來越多事情時，我們要怎麼理解它、管理它、驗證它，最後讓它變得更安全？

Pause.

> 所以這門課有一句最重要的話。

Reveal:

> **AI is the capability. Information Security is the discipline.**

Then explain briefly:

> AI 給我們能力；Security 給我們界線、方法與證據。

## Safety Foreshadow

One sentence only:

> 所有攻防實作都會在授權、隔離、可控的 Lab / TryHackMe / CTF / 自有環境中進行。

Do not expand legal or offensive-security details here.

## First Student Question

Ask:

> **如果 AI 越來越有能力，資訊安全會變得比較不重要，還是更重要？為什麼？**

Accept several answers. Do not teach the full answer yet.

Teacher closes:

> 這就是我們整學期要回答的問題。

## Transition to Slide 01

Slide 00 is the course entrance. Slide 01 now zooms into the identity statement:

> **AIIS — AI and Information Security**

Teacher transition:

> 那我們先把 AI 與 Information Security 放在同一張圖裡看。為什麼我說 AI 是 capability，而 Information Security 是 discipline？

→ Slide 01.

## Suggested Teaching Time

3–5 minutes.

Slide 00 should feel like a course opening, not a lecture-heavy page.

## Slide 00 Core Lines

> **AI is the capability. Information Security is the discipline.**

> **AI gives us more capability. Security teaches us how to trust and control that capability.**

## Curriculum Governance Note

From this point forward, every formal AIIS lesson should include a Slide 00 that establishes:
- lesson identity;
- position in the fixed curriculum;
- connection to the persistent Weather Security Center / semester story;
- lesson mission;
- transition from the previous lesson.

Slide 00 is an orientation layer and does not consume or replace the canonical lesson content allocation.
